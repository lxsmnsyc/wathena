This exception is an additional permission under section 7 of the GNU General Public License, version 3.

## Permission

A mod that interacts with wAthena only through the following interfaces is not a work based on wAthena, and may be distributed under terms of the mod author's choice:

- The public Lua API, as documented in the wAthena API reference.
- YAML data files in the formats documented by wAthena.
- Asset files, such as images, models, sounds, and maps, in the formats documented by wAthena.
- The mod manifest (`mod.yaml`).

## Limits

This exception does not apply to:

- Mods that include, copy, or modify wAthena source code.
- Mods that include scripts or data converted from rAthena. Those parts remain under the GNU General Public License, version 3, because rAthena's scripts and data are licensed under it.
- Changes to wAthena itself, including its server, client, and tools.
